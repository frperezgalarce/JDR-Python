   Variablestarsarecrucialcelestialobjectsforunderstandingthestructure
oftheuniverse,assomeofthemactasstandardcandlesfordistancemeasure-
ments(e.g.RRLyraeandCepheids).Others,suchasDeltaScutistars,play
afundamentalroleinasteroseismology,sincetheirpulsationsprovidevaluable
informationabouttheinternalstructureandevolutionarystateofstars.The
observedvariabilitycanoriginatefrominternalphysicalprocessesorexternal
causes,suchasorbitingplanetsorthepresenceofbinaryormultiplesystems.
Aparticularcaseofvariablestarscorrespondstopulsatingstars,whosebright-
nesschangesperiodicallyduetophysicaloscillationsintheirouterlayers. In
thesestars,theequilibriumbetweengravitationalcontractionandradiation
pressureisregularlydisturbed, causingcyclicexpansionsandcontractions.
Thisprocessleadstovariationsinluminosity,temperature,andradiusthat
areobservableintheirlightcurves. Relevantvariablesstarsbelongtothis
category,suchasRRLyrae,Cepheids,Mira,anddeltascuti.
   Giventheenormousamountofdatacollecteddailyfrommodernobserva-
